# ActionGate: selected product architecture

ActionGate connects a trusted workflow grant, model/API/MCP gateway, typed action
broker, inherited data labels, resource reservations and persisted audit receipts.
Approval binds the exact payload and recipient. Deterministic authorization
remains independent of the local model's content assessment.

The dated [design specification](PLAN.md) records the original requirements.
Current capabilities, deployment responsibilities and limitations are described
in the [product guide](../docs/product.md), [architecture](../docs/architecture.md)
and [operations](../docs/operations.md). Measured results are linked from
[acceptance](../docs/acceptance.md).
